# Sample shelter list and gauge triggers (exercise document; locations and thresholds are illustrative)

## Shelters (sample)
Arthur Ashe Center (north of downtown) is listed as a sample general population shelter for this exercise.
A Church Hill school shelter is listed as a sample shelter east of Shockoe Bottom.
Shelter capacity and opening status are not modelled in this exercise.

## Gauge triggers (sample)
James River at Westham (USGS gauge 02037500) is the reference gauge in this exercise.
Sample trigger: at 12 ft and rising, pre-position rescue boats on both banks and close Dock Street.
Sample trigger: at 15 ft, evacuate Shockoe Bottom ground floors.
These thresholds are invented for the exercise and are not National Weather Service flood stages.
