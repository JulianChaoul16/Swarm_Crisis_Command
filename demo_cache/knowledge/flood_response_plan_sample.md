# Sample Richmond Flood Response Plan (exercise document, not an official City of Richmond plan)

## 1 Purpose
This sample plan was written for the Crisis Command Swarm exercise to give the logistics agent something to cite. It is not an official City of Richmond plan and must not be used for real operations.

## 3.1 Life safety first
Rescue of people in moving water, trapped on roofs or in vehicles takes priority over property and infrastructure incidents.
Vulnerable people (children, elderly, disabled, injured or medically dependent) are moved first when units must choose.

## 3.2 Vehicles in water
Do not send road vehicles through flooded roads; reroute over open bridges and higher streets.
Stranded vehicles with people inside are a rescue incident; send a boat-capable unit with enough seats for everyone.

## 3.3 Low-lying districts
Shockoe Bottom, Dock Street and the south bank near Hull Street flood first; expect road closures there before the rest of downtown.
When Dock Street or East Main Street floods, use the 14th Street (Mayo) Bridge only if the road authority confirms it open.

## 4.1 Road closures
A road is treated as closed when a road authority, a field unit or confirmed imagery reports water over the road.
Hedged or second-hand reports of a closure are checked by a field unit before a route depends on them.

## 5.1 Public alerts
After a dispatch decision, issue a public alert in English and Spanish naming the areas to avoid and what to do.
Alerts use plain language and do not promise arrival times.
